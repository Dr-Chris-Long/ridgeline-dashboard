Cascade chains

Where individual findings stop being isolated issues and start compounding into the kind of systemic exposure a court treats as fatal to the record.

Single findings rarely sink a record on their own. RIDGELINE traces how they amplify each other — a baseline-data weakness undermining every downstream effects analysis; a consultation gap collapsing the authorization beneath it. The run identified nine chains, each adjudicated CRITICAL and amplified to RED. The count beside each is the breadth of findings that chain draws on; chains overlap, so the counts are not additive.

01
Mitigation Enforceability Collapse
FOUNDATION ATTACK
When the mitigation an agency relies on to keep impacts below significance is not enforceable or certain to occur, every effects conclusion resting on it loses support. Courts routinely reject a finding of no significant impact built on unenforceable mitigation.

Best Available Science Foundation
FOUNDATION ATTACK
If the scientific basis of the analysis is contested, outdated, or unaddressed, the conclusions built on it inherit the weakness. A challenge to the foundation propagates upward to every dependent determination at once.

03
Survey / Baseline Integrity Attack
FOUNDATION ATTACK
Effects analysis is only as sound as the baseline it measures against. A survey-protocol or baseline-data gap weakens every downstream impact determination simultaneously, because each one depends on the same starting picture.

04
Water Quality Foundation Attack
FOUNDATION ATTACK
Where the water-quality baseline or analysis is deficient, the aquatic, fisheries, and cumulative-effects conclusions that draw on it lose their footing — a single foundational gap with wide downstream reach.

05
ESA Consultation Collapse
SEQUENTIAL DEPENDENCY
A defect in the consultation chain — scope, currency, or a missed reinitiation trigger — can void the take authorization that downstream actions depend on. A sequential failure: one broken link disables everything beneath it.

06
Climate / Carbon Analysis Gap
CUMULATIVE WEIGHT
A thin or absent climate and carbon analysis is individually survivable, but it adds to other hard-look gaps. Collectively, these omissions can render the cumulative-effects analysis inadequate.

Multi-Statute Independent Flank
INDEPENDENT FLANK
Findings grounded in statutes beyond NEPA supply independent grounds for challenge. Even if one theory fails, an independent statutory flank can stand on its own — so the record must hold on every front, not just the strongest one.

Connected Actions Segmentation
CUMULATIVE WEIGHT

NEPA Hard-Look Cumulative Weight
CUMULATIVE WEIGHT
Several individually minor hard-look gaps aggregate. No single one is decisive, but together they can amount to a systemic failure to take the hard look NEPA requires.

Cascade types — FOUNDATION ATTACK (a base layer many findings depend on), SEQUENTIAL DEPENDENCY (one failure disables the next), INDEPENDENT FLANK (separate standalone grounds), CUMULATIVE WEIGHT (individually minor, collectively decisive).
