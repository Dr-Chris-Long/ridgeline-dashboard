Method

Coded once, traceable end to end

Every comment is decomposed through open coding into discrete codes — 1,601 in all — then consolidated into 1,109 unique concerns, each carrying a stance and a substantive-status determination. Concerns are organized into themes, giving the agency a defensible, auditable path from an individual comment to the response that addresses it.

The theme assignments shown here are model-proposed and pending human intercoder validation; reliability statistics are not yet finalized, and nothing here substitutes for the reviewer's judgment. As with the other views, project identity and all commenter and organization identifiers have been removed for this demonstration.

Pre-decisional internal analysis. Not a determination of comment adequacy or agency response. All theme assignments require human review by qualified staff before any use.
