The problem & the approach

Litigation-grade review, before the decision is signed

NEPA decisions are challenged on the adequacy of the administrative record. The vulnerabilities that decide those cases — an unsupported significance call, an unenforceable mitigation commitment, a gap in the consultation chain — are usually present in the draft long before anyone outside the agency sees them.

RIDGELINE runs the draft record through an adversarial multi-model pipeline: one model argues the challenger's case, a second mounts the agency's defense from the record, and a third adjudicates contested findings. The output is a structured, severity-ranked map of where the record is exposed — and how individual weaknesses compound into cascade chains a court would treat as systemic.

It is a pre-decisional analytical aid, not a legal opinion. Every finding is built for human review and record strengthening.
