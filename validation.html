How it works — high level

Three independent roles, one adjudicated record

ROLE 01

Challenge

An independent model argues the strongest case against the record — every weakness a sophisticated challenger would raise, with severity and confidence.

ROLE 02

Defense

A second model defends the document from the record itself, conceding nothing the administrative file can support and flagging genuine gaps.

ROLE 03

Adjudication

A third model resolves contested findings, assigns final severity, and characterizes obligations strictly from cited source text — never from memory.

draft record → challenge → defense → adjudication → severity-ranked findings + cascade map → human review
