Reading a finding

Every finding carries its own defense

A finding is not a flag — it is an adjudicated record. Each one captures the challenger's argument, the agency's defense drawn from the administrative file, the adjudicated severity, and, where warranted, a concrete fix. Findings where the file genuinely answers the challenge resolve GREEN; the analysis concedes what the record supports.

That structure is what makes the output usable for strengthening the record rather than just listing risks — and what keeps it defensible as a pre-decisional internal aid.

Pre-decisional internal review tool. Not a legal opinion and not a determination of compliance. All findings require human review by qualified staff before any use. Figures shown are real results from a de-identified prototype run; project identity has been removed for this demonstration.
